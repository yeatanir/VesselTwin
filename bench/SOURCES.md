# Benchmark sources (real_*.json)

Every text under `bench/procedures/real_*.json` is a verbatim excerpt (<= ~150 words, methods/experimental paragraph only) copied programmatically from the open-access full text served by Europe PMC (PMC open-access subset, licence checked per article as CC BY). Excerpts joined from several sentences of the same paper are separated by ` [...] `. Whitespace is normalised; nothing else is edited.

**Figures, tables, supplementary files and full articles are never redistributed.** Only the short methods excerpt is quoted, with attribution below, under the article's CC BY licence. Gold annotations are our own and cover only parameters stated in the excerpt.

Gold units are as written in the text, except that `mg cm-3` is written `mg/cm3` and `1/2 inch` is written `0.5 inch`. Categorical gold (`carrier`, `gas`, `solvent`) uses the ParamDef choice string even when the text says `argon`, `iodine` or `ultrapure water`.
