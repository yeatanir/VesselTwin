## 1. Part 1: liquid-MoO3 branch (`MoO3.psat_model = 'smolik_liquid_cc'`)
- Solid (T <= 1068 K): Smolik et al. 2000 (doi:10.2172/752007 Eq. 6) x P_ref/f(1000 K), unchanged (917.7 Pa at 1068 K with the default P_ref = 53.3 Pa).
- Liquid (T > 1068 K): Clausius-Clapeyron continuation ln p = ln p_m + B (1/1068 - 1/T), p_m = solid value at 1068 K (continuity), B = ln(101325/p_m)/(1/1068 - 1/Tb) so p(Tb) = 1 atm. Nominal B = 19929 K.
- Tb = 1428 K (`MoO3.Tb_K`): "1,155 C (2,111 F; 1,428 K)(sublimes)", CRC Handbook (Haynes, 92nd ed., p. 4.77), read on the open page https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molybdenum_trioxide (a secondary page quoting the CRC; the CRC itself not opened). Cross-check from an opened primary-data page: NIST WebBook C1313275 (https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C1313275&Mask=4) prints no boiling point but prints an Antoine fit, log10(P/bar) = A - B/(T+C), A = 2.6317, B = 2137.407, C = -616.484, 1007-1424 K, ref. "Stull, 1947" (Ind. Eng. Chem. 39, 517, doi:10.1021/ie50448a022), "coefficients calculated by NIST from author's data". Solving it for 1.01325 bar gives 1430 K (consistent). The same Antoine gives 790 Pa at 1068 K (Smolik: 918 Pa) and 32.0 kPa at 1300 K (continuation: 25.6 kPa; Fieser 30.7 kPa). It is NOT used as the law (only as a cross-check).
- Recomputed from the Edison-returned equations (Edison's quoted scale numbers were wrong): Fieser/Blackburn Eq. 43 log10(p/atm) = -7816.9/T + 5.494: 1.52 kPa at 1068 K, 30.7 kPa at 1300 K, 106 kPa at 1428 K. Blackburn Eq. 14 log10(p/atm) = -14123/T + 11.873: 4.52 kPa at 1068 K, 1.03 MPa at 1300 K, 9.7 MPa at 1428 K (not physical near Tb). Smolik's solid fit unclamped is ~19 atm at 1300 K. Neither Blackburn/Fieser relation was opened by us (Edison's description only).
- Uncertainty: MC factor `MoO3.liq_Bfac` on B, sigma 0.384 (assumed), applied only under `thermo.with_liq_sigma` so other configurations keep their MC stream. p95 slope = Blackburn Eq. 14 slope (32519 K); the p05 slope (7370 K) lies below Fieser's (18000 K), so p05-p95 contains both slopes (the anchor stays continuous at 1068 K, so the level disagreement at 1068 K is not spanned; the 1 atm pin holds only at the nominal).
- Default stays `smolik_hsc` (existing tests and stored benchmarks depend on it). Whether to flip the default is decided after scoring and stated in the results; the flip would be a one-line change in `vessels/cvd_tube/__init__.py`.
- Unit tests: continuity at 1068 K, p(Tb) = 1 atm, monotonic, solid branch unchanged below 1068 K.

## 2. Part 2: Momeni equilibrium edge model (`edge_model = 'momeni_eq'`)
Source: Momeni et al., npj 2D Mater. Appl. 2, 27 (2018), SI PDF `41699_2018_72_MOESM1_ESM.pdf`, equations read from page images: p.7 S14 gamma_AC = 0.75; S15 gamma_ZZMo = 0.2184 dmu + 1.03; S16 gamma_ZZS = -0.2041 dmu + 0.44; S17 gamma_ZZS2 = -0.4286 dmu + 0.2 (eV/A, dmu = Delta mu_S in eV); p.5 S6-S7 anisotropy; p.5 "Delta mu_S (in eV) = 0.8 - 12 u ... valid for small supersaturations" (assumed by the authors); p.4 Table S3 (Omega 3.2e-5 m3/mol, c_eq 0.4 mol/m3) and u = (c - c_eq) Omega; PDF p.10 Fig. S8 caption u_c = 0.251 (nucleation trigger, recorded, not a shape rule). The SI says the energies are "fitted from Ref. 1" (p.7) / first-principles calculations, Ref. 9 (p.4-5); the DFT level of that reference is unknown to us. Fit validity range in dmu is not stated.
- Shape: Wulff construction as the intersection of half-planes of the facet energies: ZZMo normals 0/120/240 deg, S-type zigzag 60/180/300, armchair 30 + 60k. (The cusped gamma(theta) of S7 interpolates linearly between these facets, so only the facets enter; the smoothed a(theta) of S8-S13 is a phase-field regularisation and is not used.) S-type zigzag energy = min(gamma_ZZS, gamma_ZZS2) (assumption). Energies floored at 0.01 eV/A. Shape index s = (L_Mo - L_S)/(L_Mo + L_S) (edge lengths), class by the repo thresholds (|s| < 0.15 hexagon; >= 0.95 triangle_Mo/triangle_S; else truncated_triangle). AC length share > 5 % flags a dodecagon, scored as the class the s-rule gives (the benchmark vocabulary has no dodecagon).
- **Mapping to dmu, PRIMARY (decided now): 'kT'.** dmu = Dmu_S_std_eV + 1/2 [g_S2(T) - g_S2(1073.15 K)] + 1/2 kT ln(pS2_local / 1 bar), with Dmu_S_std = -0.796 eV (MACE-MP-0 S8 ring/8 as the "bulk S" reference + JANAF S2; +-0.3 eV, MC sigma 0.3 already in the model), pS2_local and T = the Tier 1 local S2 partial pressure and wall temperature at the substrate. Reference state stated: bulk S ~ E(S8 ring)/8 (not liquid S at T).
- **SECONDARY: 'bridge'** (the authors' own): u = Omega (p_MoO3/(R T) - c_eq) with Omega, c_eq from Table S3, dmu = 0.8 - 12 u. Taken literally; with tube MoO3 pressures (<= ~1e3 Pa, c ~ 1e-1 mol/m3 << c_eq = 0.4) u ~ -1e-5 to -1e-6, so dmu ~ 0.8 eV everywhere and this map is expected to give S-triangle everywhere. That expectation is analytic, stated before scoring. It is reported, not used as a headline.
- MACE (`E_kink_*` closures), Rosen (`eq_model = dft_rosen`) and synthetic options stay available under `edge_model = 'kinetic'` and are re-run as configurations below.

## 4. Baselines and pass threshold
Baselines: blind no-tool (the existing `bench/sensitivity/blind_*.json`), always-increase (sensitivity); majority and always-triangle (shape). Metric: accuracy with abstentions (`uncertain`, band-spans-classes, outside-map) counted wrong, plus accuracy on confident calls and the abstention rate. **Pass (per item set): the model's accuracy with abstentions wrong is strictly greater than each baseline's on the same items.** Margins of one or two items are not evidence; n is 3 to 14 and all items are development data, so a pass is not a validation claim. Numbers that get worse are reported.

## Addendum B (method change AFTER the first scoring run; committed before the second run)
The first run (section 3 above, results in `bench/track_i/results.json`, `docs/track_i_results.md`) is kept as scored. After it, the coordinator supplied source PDFs and I read them. **Source findings that change the method (not blind: I have seen the first-run numbers):**
1. The SI's "Ref. 9" is Cao, Shen, Liang, Chen & Shu, J. Phys. Chem. C 119, 4294 (2015), doi:10.1021/jp5097713 (SI reference list, entry 9; the main paper's reference 10). SI p.7 says "Ref. 1" for S14-S17, which is a numbering slip (SI ref. 1 is Reid & Sherwood); Fig. S9 caption says "drawn based on the expressions and parameters in Ref. 9". DFT level, from the paper itself (p.4296): VASP, PAW, GGA-PBE, plane-wave cutoff 400 eV, forces < 0.01 eV/A, free-standing monolayer, ZPE included; computed heat of formation 2.64 eV; allowed range mu_S(bulk) - dHf/2 < mu_S < mu_S(bulk), i.e. **dmu_S between -1.32 and 0 eV**. The authors' bridge dmu = 0.8 - 12 u gives +0.8 eV at u = 0, outside that range.
2. Cao (p.4296): "ZZ-S and ZZ-Mo2 have the same edge orientation, and the orientation of ZZ-Mo and ZZ-S2 edges is the same"; ZZ-S2 = Mo edge with S termination. So the registered rule "S-type zigzag = min(gamma_ZZS, gamma_ZZS2)" put ZZS2 on the wrong orientation. **Corrected rule (v2): Mo-orientation energy = min(gamma_ZZMo, gamma_ZZS2); S-orientation energy = gamma_ZZS.** Implementation check against Cao's printed text (not against the benchmark): printed "dodecagon at dmu -1.32 to -0.98, hexagon -0.98 to -0.04, triangle beyond -0.04, ZZ-S2 most stable above ~-1.1, ZZ-S below": v2 gives AC edges until about -1.05, a hexagon (|s| < 0.15) up to -0.9, and the triangle at -0.04; v1 does not reproduce this.
3. `edge_model = 'momeni_eq'` now means v2; the first-registered version stays reachable as `'momeni_eq_v1'`. New configurations: **M5 = momeni_eq (v2), kT mapping (primary mapping unchanged)**, M6 = momeni_eq (v2), bridge. M3/M4 (v1) were scored in the first run and stay in the tables as registered. Second run scores only: M5, M6 on the shape benchmark (n = 14, 8 in scope) and s11, plus the checks D1, D2 below. Same metric, baselines, pass rule (strictly above always-triangle with abstentions wrong). M5/M6 are not blind to the benchmark, so even a pass would not be a validation.
4. **Check D1 (development; Momeni 2018 own experiment).** Source: main paper p.3 "~10 um-sized hexagonal MoS2 (Fig. 3g) form at the leading edge of the substrate (spot #1 in Fig. 3c) changes to quasi-hexagonal, 3(h), (spot #2 in Fig. 3c) and then to triangles, 3(i), (spot #3 in Fig. 3c)"; SI Table S4 average edges per island (experiment): ~6, ~6, 3.3 for regions i, ii, iii; text p.3 "S-rich atmosphere (or low concentration of MoO3 precursor) favors the triangular shape while the Mo-rich atmosphere (or high concentration of MoO3 precursor) favors the more isotropic dodecagonal island shapes". Conditions (SI p.7 / main Methods): 800 C co-heated MoO3 (2 mg) and face-down SiO2/Si substrate 0.5 cm downstream of the MoO3, S (200 mg) heat tape 130 C ~15 cm upstream of the substrate, 100 sccm Ar, 710 Torr, 40 mm tube, 15 min. Model setup: `T_boat_Mo_C = T_furnace_C = 800`, `T_boat_S_C = 130`, `tube_id_in = 1.575`, `flow_sccm = 100`, `pressure_Pa = 710 x 133.322 = 94658`, `duration_min = 15`, `m_MoO3_mg = 2`, `m_S_mg = 200`, `x_MoO3_cm = 50`, `x_S_boat_cm = 35.5`, `x_substrate_cm = 0.5`. Substrate length and spot positions are not given as numbers; **assumption: leading edge = 0.5 cm and downstream spot = 1.5 cm downstream of the MoO3 boat** (the phase-field window is 3.6 mm; the true spot spacing is unknown). Call: paired MC change (60 draws, seed 0, `core.uq.sample`) of |s| from 0.5 to 1.5 cm: `more_triangular` if p05 > 0.05, `less_triangular` if p95 < -0.05, `no_change` if both |p05|,|p95| < 0.05, else `uncertain`. Correct iff `more_triangular`. Run for M0, M1, M2 (kinetic), M3 (v1), M5, M6.
5. **Check D2 (development; Wang et al. 2014).** Source: Chem. Mater. 26, 6371, p.6374-6375: for Mo:S > 1:2 "S-zz terminations grow faster than the Mo-zz terminations ... the domain shape will change from a hexagon to a triangle with three sides of Mo-zz terminations"; at the stoichiometric ratio "the final shape of the domains will be hexagon"; for an S-rich atmosphere the shape "will also transform to triangular but having three sides of S-zz terminations". Directional test: from the default tube at 0.5 cm... at a fixed (pMoO3 = 10 Pa, T = 1073 K) raising pS2 from 10 to 1000 Pa should move the shape index s from positive (Mo-zz triangle) toward negative (S-zz triangle). Call = sign of s(1000 Pa) - s(10 Pa) (`decrease` expected; `no_change` if |change| < 0.05). Run for the same configurations. Note the conflict between sources: Cao's equilibrium shape at S-rich is the ZZ-S2 (Mo-orientation) triangle, i.e. s -> +1, the opposite orientation to Wang's S-rich S-zz triangle. Both reported.
6. **Nanomaterials 2024, 14, 1749 (Esposito et al.)**: opened; MoS2 from a spin-coated ammonium-heptamolybdate solution (not a MoO3 vapour boat), S boat 180 C, 820 C, N2 150-300 sccm; "average flake sizes ... 5.6 +- 0.7, 19.8 +- 2.9, 12.0 +- 3.5, and 76.7 +- 9.1 um for flows rates of 150, 200, 250 and 300 sccm". Not added as an item: the model has no handle for a spin-coated Mo source (its Mo flux is a Hertz-Knudsen boat that responds to flow; here the Mo is on the substrate) and the series is non-monotonic. Recorded as excluded (no model handle).
7. **Samant et al. 1992 (J. Alloys Compd. 187, 373)**: opened. Solid MoO3 only, 947-1048 K ("vapour pressure of MoO3(s)"); Table 1 total pressure, this work (Pa): 947 K 5.08; 998 K 55.08; 1048 K 440.72 (Brewer-Lamoreaux 4.86 / 52.41 / 437.77). Brewer-Lamoreaux equations are "valid for the temperature range 800-1074 K". **No liquid-MoO3 equation or table**, so Part 1 stays as registered. The Smolik solid law in the model gives 4.5 / 48.8 / 411 Pa at those temperatures (-11 to -6 % vs this work): a solid-branch cross-check, no change to any constant.
