# Track N pre-registration (committed before any scoring)

Branch `track-n` (from origin/polish 3de09fc). Written Oct 21 2026 after the furnace module and its unit tests were written and after the stored CFD columns (`bench/track_n/cfd_extract.json`) and the digitised Momeni figures (`bench/track_n/momeni_digitized.json`) were extracted, and before `bench/track_n_score.py --score` was run. During development I looked at ONE stored CFD case (`v5agree_default`, mesh columns only) to check the extractor; I have not compared any model output with any CFD or published value. The scoring run is executed once after this commit.

## 0. What can and cannot be tested
The stored OpenFOAM tube cases impose the legacy wall-temperature profile as a Dirichlet condition on the inner wall, boats (2.5 mm rings) and substrate (fixed-T wall strips), use constant mu (at T_furnace) and constant Pr and Cp, and **have gravity switched off** (`constant/g = (0 0 0)`, axisymmetric wedge). So the CFD can test only the **gas-side** part of the furnace model (N3: gas heating, centreline versus bulk, expansion velocity). It cannot test the quartz network (N2), the boat/substrate temperature balance (N4), or whether buoyancy matters (the CFD has none). The model and the CFD share several assumptions (prescribed wall T, no radiation); a pass is a consistency check of the energy solver and of the velocity bookkeeping, not a validation against a real furnace. Each of the 52 CFD cases is the same code and physics, so the cases are not independent evidence.

## 1. Model under test (`vessels/cvd_tube/furnace.py`), fixed before scoring
- `gas_model = energy_2d` (headline): steady axisymmetric energy equation, prescribed Poiseuille mass-flux profile, axial + radial conduction, T-dependent k (Picard), hybrid axial scheme, inlet Dirichlet T_ends, outlet zero gradient, wall Dirichlet. Grid nx = clip(L_tube/0.2 cm, 300, 1000), nr = 28.
- `gas_props = auto` (headline; NIST argon table, opened) and `cfd_const` (diagnostic: the CFD's constant mu, Pr, Cp at T_furnace, isolates the property mismatch from structural error).
- `gas_model = graetz_1d` (Hausen developing-flow Nusselt march, `Nu` = 3.66) is scored as a second variant.
- Wall input for the CFD comparison: the wall temperature **as imposed in each CFD case** (piecewise constant per mesh cell, including the boat and substrate strips), `quartz_coupling` not involved (the gas solver is called with that wall).
- Legacy comparator: `vessels.cvd_tube.march` with `thermal_model = legacy` on the same values (its cell-mean gas temperature `Tg`; velocity from its own ideal-gas bookkeeping u = N R Tg / (P A); legacy has no radial information, so its centreline prediction is its mean).
- No parameter is tuned. All values are the ParamDef defaults except the case values stored with each CFD case.

## 2. Test (a): stored OpenFOAM tube cases
Cases: the 12 `v5agree_*` cases (default, boat650/750/800, boatS150, flow50/200/400, sub6, sub20, furn700, furn850) and the 40 `v5sweep_00..39` cases (tube diameters up to 2.2 in, flows up to ~900 sccm). Inclusion (fixed now): extraction succeeded; the probe temperature changed by less than 2 K over the last 20 % of simulated time (settled); both stations lie inside the tube. Cases that fail inclusion are listed, not scored.
Stations (from the CFD mesh geometry `tier2.openfoam._tube_geo`): **boat** = centre of the MoO3 boat strip, **substrate** = substrate centre (primary, scored), **release** station (3 cm downstream of the boat; secondary, reported).
CFD quantities per station (cell-volume weights in a column): mixing-cup T (rho u_x weighted, rho = p M / R T), axis-cell T, area-mean u_x.
Metrics per case and primary station:
1. M1 absolute error of the bulk gas temperature (model T_gas_mean versus CFD mixing-cup), K;
2. M2 absolute error of the centreline temperature (model T_gas_centre versus CFD axis cell; legacy predicts its mean), K;
3. M3 relative error of the area-mean velocity (|u_model / u_CFD - 1|);
4. M4 Richardson-number regime: Ri = Gr/Re^2 = g (dT/T_film) d / u^2 with dT = |T_wall - T_gas_mean| at each station and the maximum over the boat-to-substrate span; the CFD value uses the CFD's own T_mix and u_mean (and the imposed wall T), the model value is the model's Gr/Re^2 with its own properties. Reported: agreement of the flag Ri > Ri_c = 1 (stations and span maximum) and the ratio model/CFD of the span maximum (pass: within a factor 2). The CFD has no gravity, so M4 compares the **number and flag from the CFD fields**, not a buoyant flow.
**Pass threshold** (fixed now): a case-station passes M1 and M2 if the error is <= 25 K, M3 if <= 15 %, M4 if the flags agree and the span-maximum ratio is within a factor 2. A case passes overall if M1, M2, M3 pass at both primary stations. Reported: number of passing cases for energy_2d (auto and cfd_const), graetz_1d and legacy (M1, M3 for legacy; M2 for legacy as defined above), medians and maxima of each error, the per-case table, and the cases where legacy has the smaller error (counted separately for M1, M2, M3). With n <= 52 the pass fractions are descriptive; no significance claim.
**Prediction made before scoring:** energy_2d with NIST properties should be within 25 K of the CFD in most cases at both stations, because both solve the same energy equation; the main sources of difference are the constant-property CFD thermodynamics (the diagnostic variant should be closer), axial conduction through the discretisation, and the prescribed-profile assumption near the hot ramp. Legacy should be good for the bulk temperature (stored comparison: T_gas CFD/Tier 1 0.982-1.001) and unable to predict the centreline lag, so energy_2d should win on M2. Velocity is expected to pass for both.

## 3. Test (b): Momeni et al. 2018 (published simulation, not a measurement)
Input: the furnace wall profile digitised from SI Fig. S2 (`bench/track_n/momeni_digitized.json`, 18 points, reading uncertainty +-5 C interior, +-20 C and +-15 C the two extrapolated ends), as `wall_profile_table`, tube inner diameter 40 mm (stated), 100 sccm Ar (stated), 710 Torr (stated), inlet temperature T_ends = 25 C (assumed; the simulation's own inlet was 655 K, not stated how), L_tube = 120 cm, `quartz_coupling = off` (the figure is the 'furnace wall', imposed as the model's wall) and `on` (reported). The x origin of the two figures differs; alignment (fixed now, uses no model output): the S2 profile starts at 550 C so its rising edge is cut off; the mid-height position of the FALLING edge (half of max-min) of the wall profile and that of the S7 curve are aligned, inches converted to cm (1 in = 2.54 cm).
Implementation details fixed before scoring (not in the first draft): outside the digitised x range the wall is ramped linearly to 25 C over 15 cm on each side; the sulfur heat belt rule is switched off (`S_heat_belt = off`); the extra heat-belt edge smoothing of `furnace.twall` (1 cm erf) applies only to the parametric profile.
Comparison curve: SI Fig. S7(b), the **simulated** (COMSOL) gas temperature along the axis, digitised (+-3 K, +-0.2 cm), and the main text value Tmax = 1050 K. Metrics: (i) peak gas temperature difference, model minus S7, K; pass if <= 50 K in magnitude (looser than test (a) because the reference is a simulation of unknown fidelity and the profile is read from a figure); (ii) RMS difference over the heated region (S7 T > 800 K); pass if <= 50 K. The 'slight drop of about 4 K near the crucible' is not testable (the model has no crucible geometry); it is stated as not tested. Legacy comparator: the legacy Graetz lag formula (constant Nu = 3.66, k(T) of the carrier, as in `march`) applied to the same wall table.
**Prediction made before scoring:** this comparison will most likely FAIL by about 100-150 K: the digitised wall profile peaks near 918-921 C (about 1192 K) while the S7 gas peaks at 1050 K, and at Re ~ 2 in a 40 mm tube every gas model I know of (including this one) closes the gas to the wall temperature within centimetres, so the model's plateau gas T will be near the wall's 1190 K. The source also states a 800 C growth temperature for the same experiment, and its S7 gas floor (655 K) is hard to reconcile with an S2 wall that falls to about 65 C at its right end. If it fails, the failure is reported with these inconsistencies as candidate explanations, none of which is tested here; no constant is changed to improve it.

## 4. Test (c): published MEASURED gas or substrate temperatures in a CVD tube
Searched (web search, Valency semantic search, local PDFs `Prompts/SI_data/ask`): no paper with a described furnace, flow and a **measured** in-tube gas or substrate temperature was found whose text I could open. Candidates examined: Deep & Jagadeesh, Rev. Sci. Instrum. 90, 074903 (2019), doi 10.1063/1.5088149 (two-colour pyrometry of a steel sheet at the centre of a 1000 mm sillimanite tube furnace: 979.5 K +- 24 % for a 1000 K wall and 1391 K +- 6.7 % for 1426 K) - only the abstract is accessible (paywall), tube diameter, flow and gas unknown, so it is NOT scored; arXiv:1811.06119 (Fig. 1c, schematic temperature-versus-time of MoO3 and S, no measurement method stated) - not scored; Notthoff et al. 2013 (LIF gas thermometry in a hot-wall CVS reactor, abstract only) - not scored. This is stated as a gap: **the furnace model's absolute temperatures (quartz, boat, substrate) are not validated against any measurement.**
