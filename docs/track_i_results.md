- Caveats on the model itself: Momeni's dmu is "between bulk S atoms and S atoms in MoS2"; our dmu uses E(S8 ring)/8 as the bulk-S reference (+-0.3 eV, MACE-MP-0), an unverified conversion. The DFT level behind their Ref. 9 energies is unknown to us. u_c = 0.251 is a nucleation threshold, unused.

## Addendum B: sources supplied after the first run, corrected edge orientation (second run, `bench/track_i/results_addendum.json`)
Method change after seeing results, pre-registered in Addendum B (committed before this run); both results are reported. All development data; M5/M6 are not blind.
**What the sources say.** SI "Ref. 9" is Cao, Shen, Liang, Chen & Shu, J. Phys. Chem. C 119, 4294 (2015) (SI reference list entry 9). DFT level, p.4296: VASP, PAW, GGA-PBE, 400 eV cutoff, forces < 0.01 eV/A, free-standing monolayer, ZPE included, dHf 2.64 eV; allowed dmu_S -1.32 to 0 eV. Our dmu from the kT map lies in that range (about -1.33 to -0.9 eV); the authors' bridge gives +0.8 eV at u = 0, outside it. Cao: "ZZ-Mo and ZZ-S2 ... same orientation"; my first registered rule put ZZS2 on the S-edge orientation, which was wrong. v2 (Mo orientation = min(ZZMo, ZZS2), S orientation = ZZS) reproduces Cao's printed shape sequence (AC edges at -1.2 eV, none at -1.0; hexagon at -0.95; triangle at -0.04); v1 did not. SI p.7 labels the fit source "Ref. 1" (a slip).

Default-tube map under M5: 19 hexagon + 21 truncated_triangle positions, boundary at the map start (no triangle zone); M6: 40 triangle_Mo. s11: M3 uncertain, M5 uncertain, M6 no_change.
**D1, Momeni's own 800 C experiment** (hexagon at the leading edge -> quasi-hexagon -> triangles downstream; edges ~6, ~6, 3.3, Table S4; spot positions assumed 0.5 and 1.5 cm from the MoO3 boat). Correct only if |s| rises downstream. Result, paired MC change of |s|: M0, M1, M2 (kinetic): s = +1 at both positions (triangle_Mo, no change): wrong. M3 v1: s -0.178 -> -0.178, no_change: wrong. **M5 v2: s = -0.166 -> -0.166 (truncated-triangle/hexagon boundary, matches the leading-edge/quasi-hexagon end of the sequence) but no change downstream**: no_change, wrong. M6: +1 both: wrong. 0/6 correct. Cause: the kT map depends on pS2 and T only, and neither varies over 1 cm in the tube model, whereas the paper's downstream trend is driven by falling MoO3 concentration; the equilibrium map has no Mo-supply dependence (the bridge has it, but u is ~0 for the model's MoO3 pressures).
**D2, Wang 2014 direction** (Mo:S > 1:2 gives Mo-zz triangle, S-rich gives S-zz triangle; so s should fall as pS2 rises 10 -> 1000 Pa). M0, M1, M2: s = 1 at both (no_change): wrong. M3 v1: -0.264 -> -0.612 (decrease: correct, but v1 has the wrong edge orientation, so this is accidental). **M5 v2: -0.107 -> +0.032 (increase): wrong** because Cao's S-rich equilibrium triangle is the ZZ-S2 (Mo-orientation) one, opposite to Wang's S-rich S-zz triangle; the two sources disagree on orientation (the benchmark merges both triangles, so this never showed up in the accuracy tables). M6: no_change.
**Other sources.** Wang 2014 also reports (700 C, MoO3 boat, S 18 cm upstream, 1 in tube) along the substrate: truncated triangles near the MoO3, then triangles, truncated triangles, hexagons (~10 mm from the MoO3), small triangles: non-monotonic; MoO3 at 650 C gave three-point stars; no sulfur-supply series. Nanomaterials 2024 (Esposito): spin-coated liquid Mo precursor, flows 150-300 sccm, sizes 5.6, 19.8, 12.0, 76.7 um: no model handle (Mo source is on the substrate), not added; so there is still no new held-out item. Samant 1992 (opened): solid MoO3 only, 947-1048 K, no liquid equation, so Part 1 is unchanged; its Table 1 total pressures (5.08, 55.08, 440.72 Pa at 947, 998, 1048 K) agree with the Smolik solid law in the model (4.5, 48.8, 411 Pa) to -6 to -11 %.
